Design Rules

Six universal principles that guide every application of the system.

✅ Always Do

  • 🎨Assign each product a primary gradient
  • ⚪Use white/off-white interiors for all cards
  • 👑Base all membership on Twilight
  • 🪙Always use Sunrise for goCOINS
  • 🚨Let Sunset override other gradients
  • 💪Give lead gradient 70% visual weight

❌ Never Do

  • 🚫Combine 3+ gradients in one context
  • 🚫Use Twilight for operational messages
  • 🚫Put dark text directly on gradients
  • 🚫Create new gradients beyond these six
  • 🚫Use Sunrise for mandatory actions
  • 🚫Mix property tiers without hierarchy
